Your music, the way it was recorded.

Cadence is a music player for the files you own. It plays FLAC and other lossless formats bit-perfect, keeps albums gapless, follows every word of the lyrics, and turns your library into mixes by how the songs sound.

Download Cadence Source code on GitHub

Free and open source. Windows, macOS, Linux and Android.

What happens to the sound

  1. Your fileFLAC 24-bit / 96 kHz
  2. Sound CheckOff
  3. EqualizerOff
  4. Direct output96 kHz, 24-bit
  5. Your DACBit-perfect

Cadence shows what happens to every song on its way to your speakers. When nothing changes it, it says so.

Hear the file, not the player

Direct output sends each song to your device at its own sample rate, past the system mixer, so 24-bit masters arrive sample for sample. Turn on Sound Check and every song plays at the same loudness, measured with ReplayGain 2.0, and a gentle per-song tone keeps quieter songs full without pushing the voice back.

  • Gapless playback, and crossfade between albums if you like
  • Ten-band and parametric equalizer, with headphone profiles from AutoEq
  • Crossfeed for headphones, high-quality resampling when it's needed

Sing along, word by word

Synced lyrics light up as they're sung, like karaoke. Turn the vocals down with one slider and sing the song yourself. Cadence reads lyrics from your files, finds timed ones online, or lets the AI DJ look them up, and remembers them for next time.

Mixes by how your music sounds

Cadence listens to each song once, on your computer, for its tempo, timbre, loudness and harmony. That powers sound radio from any song, lists of songs that sound alike, and mixes it builds and names for you.

Want more? Connect an AI DJ (Z.ai GLM or any compatible service, with your own key) and ask for "calm songs for late-night coding", get daily mixes, and find new music to buy.

Everything else you'd expect

  • Your library, your folders

    Add folders or drop them on the window. Cadence watches them and picks up new music by itself.

  • Media keys and mini player

    Play from the keyboard, the system media controls or a small always-on-top player. On Android: lock screen controls and a home screen widget.

  • Playlists and smart playlists

    Build them by hand, or with rules like "played often, not this month".

  • Edit song info

    Fix titles, artists and albums in the files themselves.

  • Scrobbling

    Keep a record of what you listen to on ListenBrainz.

  • Formats

    FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, MP3, AAC and Ogg Vorbis, up to 32-bit and 384 kHz.

Your music stays yours

Cadence has no account and no tracking. Sound analysis runs on your computer. Lyrics lookups send only the artist and title. The AI DJ is off until you add your own key, and Settings shows exactly what it would send.
